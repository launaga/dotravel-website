Rambu Solo:
A Torajan Farewell

20–26 August 2026 · 7 days · maximum 8 travellers

The journey

Arrive with context. Leave with a relationship to the place.

Rambu Solo is a ceremony of remembrance and responsibility. This journey is timed with a host family in Tana Toraja and moves at their pace. Attendance is never guaranteed or staged; when we are welcomed, we arrive as guests, with a local guide who explains what is happening and how to take part respectfully.

The rest of the week gives the highlands room to speak for themselves: stone graves, market mornings, coffee gardens, and long drives through valleys cut by rice terraces.

Your seven days

Day 1
Makassar to the highlands

Meet your journey host and fly north to Palopo before the mountain drive into Toraja.

Day 2
Reading the landscape

A slow first day through traditional villages, cliff graves, and rice fields with a Torajan cultural guide.

Days 3–4
Ceremony window

The schedule remains flexible around the host family's invitation and local ceremony calendar.

Day 5
North Toraja on foot

Walk between mountain villages and share lunch in a family home.

Day 6
Coffee and open roads

Visit a small highland coffee garden, then keep the afternoon unplanned.

Day 7
Return to Makassar

Drive down from the highlands and connect with your onward flight.

What is included

Six nights in handpicked stays, breakfast daily, four lunches and three dinners, private transport, domestic flight from Makassar, a dedicated journey host, local specialist guides, contributions requested by host communities, and 24/7 trip support.

Before you decide

This route includes long mountain drives, uneven paths, and moments where photography is not appropriate. We brief every traveller before arrival and adapt the daily plan with our hosts.
